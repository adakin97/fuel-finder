    The average litre rose by about 20p in March, which the RAC called the biggest monthly increase on record, as war in the Middle East pushed up the price of oil. Prices peaked at {{spring_peak}}p in late May and eased to {{summer_low}}p in early July. They have risen by {{second_rise}}p since then, and the average has not fallen on any day since {{last_fall}}. Petrol is now {{above_peak}}p above the May peak, though still about {{record_gap}}p below the record set in the summer of 2022.

    How we did this

    • Prices come from Fuel Finder, the government scheme that requires forecourts to report price changes within 30 minutes. This page uses standard unleaded (E10) only.
    • Each forecourt's latest price is carried forward day by day until it reports again, for up to 30 days, so averages are taken across forecourts rather than across price updates. Changing that window moves the 1 October average between {{cap_lo}}p and {{cap_hi}}p.
    • Most forecourts joined the scheme in late January, so the 1 February figure covers fewer of them. On the {{matched_n}} forecourts with a price on both dates, the rise is {{matched_rise}}p.
    • We removed {{dropped_rows}} of {{raw_rows}} price records: reports dated before 2026, second reports at the same timestamp, and prices that could not be real, such as prices entered in pounds rather than pence and 999.9p placeholders. We checked every forecourt's map position against its postcode and corrected {{coords_fixed}}.
    • As a check, our averages for 1 and 31 March ({{mean_1mar}}p and {{mean_31mar}}p) compare with the RAC's {{rac_1mar}}p and {{rac_31mar}}p.
    • A "motorway" site is one flagged as such by its operator. Some flagged sites are on A-roads, and the median is less affected by them than the average. Brand and area comparisons exclude motorway sites.
    • A forecourt only has to report when its price changes, so some prices are out of date. The cheapest-near-you result uses only prices reported in the last {{fresh_days}} days, and older prices are marked. It also leaves out Costco, which sells only to members; Costco still appears in the list.
    • Postcode searches use postcodes.io. Distances are straight-line. A tank is 55 litres, the RAC's standard for a family car.

    Sources: Fuel Finder, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (Open Government Licence v3.0); RAC; postcodes.io
