    How we did this

    • Prices come from Fuel Finder, the government scheme that has required forecourts to report price changes within 30 minutes since early 2026. This analysis uses standard unleaded (E10) only.
    • Each forecourt's latest price is carried forward day by day until it reports again (for up to 30 days), so averages are taken across forecourts rather than across price updates.
    • We removed {{dropped_rows}} of {{raw_rows}} price records: duplicates, impossible dates and prices that were clearly typing errors, such as 1.5p or 999.9p. {{coords_fixed}} forecourts with misplaced map coordinates were corrected using their postcode.
    • As a check, our average of {{mean_1mar}}p on 1 March is {{rac_diff}}p from the RAC's published figure of {{rac_1mar}}p for that day.
    • A "motorway" site is one flagged as such by its operator. Some of those are on major A-roads, so we use the median price for that comparison. Brand and area comparisons exclude motorway sites.
    • Postcode searches use postcodes.io. Distances are straight-line. The cheapest-near-you result leaves out Costco, which sells only to members. A tank is 55 litres, the RAC's standard for a family car.
